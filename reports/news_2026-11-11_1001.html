Global gold price drop 2026: Yellow metal slips 6% in dollar terms, but top Indian gold ETFs still deliver 7.5% returns

Livemint · 11 Nov, 09:18 IST

Global gold prices dropped by over 6% in 2026, with TradingView data showing a year-to-date return of -6.36% and trading around $4,045 per troy ounce on 11 November. A weaker Indian rupee against the US dollar cushions falling international gold prices by making imports more expensive, supporting domestic prices. Quantum Mutual Fund reported that gold ended 30 October at $3,875 per troy ounce, marking a monthly decline of about 6.87% and falling nearly 30% below January's record high.

Top performing Indian gold ETFs delivered positive returns this year exceeding 7.5%, with 360 ONE Gold ETF yielding 7.54% and Choice Gold ETF yielding 6.68% as of 9 November. Quantum Mutual Fund stated that October's 6.87% correction was driven by rising oil prices, persistent inflation, tighter US monetary policy expectations, and elevated real bond yields. Quantum Mutual Fund reported that global gold ETFs saw cumulative net inflows of $1.2 billion through 23 October and aggregate holdings increased by about 10 tonnes in October.

Quantum Mutual Fund stated that government debt, global economic uncertainty, and geopolitical tensions could continue to support gold demand, despite price corrections. Livemint ↗
